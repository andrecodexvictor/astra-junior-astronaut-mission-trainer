# ASTRA demo spatial plan

This is an authored, fictional training layout, not lunar topography. Coordinates are meters, Y up, with each independent region centered on its own origin. The manifest in `src/world/manifest.ts` is the editable marker export and spatial baseline. The player occupies approximately 0.6 m width; routes are open flat areas wider than 1.8 m. Region limits are solid controller bounds. Walk speed is 2.5 m/s, sprint 4.5 m/s, jump speed 2.4 m/s and lunar gravity 1.62 m/s² (ideal lunar apex 1.78 m). Academy uses Earth gravity of 9.81 m/s²; the simplified, floor-assisted orbital trainer also uses Earth gravity and does not simulate free fall. No required route depends on a jump, ledge or gap.

The academy runs from the south entry to a north launch, past a side console. Orbit places cargo beside the central aisle and the transfer at the north. The habitat is a radial hub: life-support equipment sits beside the central aisle, greenhouse to the east and expedition exit north. The canyon gives a clear central route with an optional right branch to Scout and a left prospect, then the beacon to the north; its return portal is at the south entry. The greenhouse has planting beds along both sides of a central aisle. Portal IDs are stable in the manifest; transitions are decided by the application.

Large authored structures and selected equipment have controller collision footprints; boundary limits prevent departure from each finite region. Smaller rocks and decorative infrastructure are landmarks without collision. Ground and scenery are artistic simplifications. Browser traversal, native captures, collision and camera verification remain pending; the automated spatial tests do not establish device performance or visual quality.
