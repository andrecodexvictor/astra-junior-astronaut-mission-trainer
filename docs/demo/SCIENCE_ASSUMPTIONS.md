# Demo science and simulation assumptions

ASTRA is an independent educational prototype, not an official submission, astronaut protocol or validated engineering model. Geography, rates and hazards are fictional gameplay choices, not NASA operational values. The intended learning cycle is inspect, propose a hypothesis, intervene, observe evidence and resource trends, and reflect. A recorded hypothesis is not a graded answer or proof of learning.

## Time and systems

The simulation uses ordered one-second ticks, each representing 0.01 mission hours; rates below are per modeled mission hour. Faster game time advances all resources and hazards. Paused panels and hidden tabs pause progression rather than catching up from wall time. The simulation state is version 3; the local save envelope is version 4, with migration paths for envelopes 1–3. Checkpoint recovery restores saved state, and validation is not cryptographic anti-tamper protection.

- **Energy:** generation and demand are in kW, stores and flows in kWh. The lunar battery holds up to 12 kWh; charging and discharging are each 90% efficient, with losses and curtailed surplus recorded. A live power module supplies a baseline 5 kW, reduced to 45% during an active storm. Actual demand depends on live modules and ongoing work. An unmet demand is a blackout.
- **Life support:** crew consumption is an aggregate model (including Cometa when active), not individual human or veterinary metabolism: 0.18 kg/h potable water, 0.08 kg/h oxygen and 0.025 kg/h food. Used water enters wastewater. A powered live recycler processes up to 0.12 kg/h, returning 80% to potable water and recording the rest as loss. Powered electrolysis consumes up to 0.18 kg/h water and yields oxygen and hydrogen in the ideal 8:1 mass ratio. Inputs are limited by available stocks.
- **Ice and food:** the finite 8 kg ice deposit supplies a 0.5 kg carried sample, which is delivered and then processed with power into potable water. Delivered and processed totals are cumulative counters, not extra reservoirs. A planted crop needs power, a live crop module and water for 180 ticks; irrigation draws up to 0.012 kg/h and is tracked as crop water. Harvest is an abstract yield: biomass, carbon and full plant mass balance are not modeled.
- **Radiation:** the deterministic game storm warns at lunar tick 180 and is active at ticks 300–379 in each 600-tick cycle. Exposure outside shelter reduces health; a field-kit shield changes that penalty. Neither timing nor the protection model is a real forecast, radiation dose calculation or shielding certification.

## Equipment and companions

A standard repair requires 0.2 kWh and five ticks. Scout repair consumes one spare part, 0.3 kWh and five ticks; ongoing work also adds demand. A Scout scan consumes 0.4 kWh from its own battery and can leave it stranded at critical charge; recharge transfers up to 0.5 kWh from the main battery, subject to availability and its 2 kWh capacity. The base also transfers charge to eligible companions during powered, non-expedition ticks. Telemetry describes game state and fictional discoveries, not verified lunar measurements.

The habitat auxiliary bay has power, water and oxygen ports. Installing each costs 0.15 kWh and requires its parent module connected; removal does not refund that cost. When the corresponding parent is live, the power port adds 0.5 kW baseline solar capacity, while the water and oxygen ports increase processing rates by 0.04 kg/h and demand by 0.15 and 0.2 kW respectively.

The three-slot field kit accepts a relay mast, thermal shield and sensor pylon. Each placement reserves 0.2 kWh; removal or undo releases the recorded escrow, restoring energy up to battery capacity and recording any excess as curtailed. The relay affects PIP's modeled telemetry range, the shield affects storm exposure and baseline demand, and the sensor adds modeled solar capacity. These are bounded game rules, not real device specifications. Cometa can follow, wait, be recalled, be petted, sniff in permitted pressurized areas or look at visible beacons; these actions do not create resources or complete quests. The aggregate consumption does not model her separate physiology.

The presentation layer enforces world-target proximity; the pure simulation checks phase, region, resources and prerequisites but does not independently know player coordinates. `src/simulation/index.ts`, `src/app/save.ts` and typed tests in `tests/unit/simulation.test.ts` and `tests/unit/app.test.ts` define and exercise current behavior. Consult `TEST_REPORT.md` for actual recorded test results. No human play duration, learning outcome, device performance or real-world safety claim follows from these model assumptions.
