# Pitch handoff

`PITCH_EN.json` is the main 30-slide, 180-second English narrative. `ELEVATOR_PT.json` is the explicitly requested Portuguese exception, six slides and 30 seconds. Each slide has `id`, `stage`, `durationSeconds`, `headline`, `subtitle`, `narration`, `visualBrief`, `art`, `layout`, `status`, and `sourceIds`. Art paths resolve relative to this directory. Layout is `fullbleed` or `evidence`; status is intended to be visible, including concept, future and evidence limits. `SOURCES_EN.md` resolves the IDs and explains claims.

Render whole scene images integrated as backgrounds, with legible text and a visible status; do not isolate HUD elements. The concept boards are generated production visions and must be credited/labeled as generated concepts, not mistaken for the playable demo. The fixture screenshots are staged software-rendered scenes. Preserve attribution and source limitations in speaker notes or an accessible companion, with short source references visible on evidence slides. The final invitation is to evaluate the playable demo and co-design a proposed supervised educator pilot; there is no established pilot or registration channel implied.

The public demo URL documented in the prior pitch is https://astra-mission-trainer.andrevictorandrade.chatgpt.site. Its exact present availability was not checked in this writing pass. Rehearse all narration at the assigned slide duration and check the rendered type and scene framing before delivery. The 30-second format is a creative target, not a verified 2026 submission rule.
