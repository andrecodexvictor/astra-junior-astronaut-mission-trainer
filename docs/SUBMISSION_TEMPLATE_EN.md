## Short summary — design intent only

ASTRA proposes a scientific exploration RPG in which learners investigate a lunar outpost with original robotic companions. Field decisions connect power, water, oxygen, food and qualitative radiation safety. Players observe evidence, test an explanation, act, measure the consequences and reflect. Short character scenes support exploration rather than replace it.
